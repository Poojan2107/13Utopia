# Suggestions — not the brief

Use only if it makes the brief better. Until chosen, **[BRIEF.md](./BRIEF.md) wins.**

---

## S1. Category name (open in brief)

**Public:** Creative technology & growth company — CEOs parse it in one beat.  
**Inside the brand:** Collective — people, culture, careers.

One ownable line later. Do not ship two competing category names on the homepage.

## S2. GROW the world vs Grow the outcome

Brief keeps both. Fine. In type and IA, never let them collide:

- World: **GROW** (always with CREATE / BUILD)  
- Outcome: **Grow** among Launch / Scale / … — or spoken as “grow demand / grow revenue” in body, not as a lone word in the same size as the world.

Do not rename the outcome unless it actually confuses in a prototype.

## S3. Two journeys, one nav

Brief wants “I need to…” and “I want to…”. Putting both in the **primary bar** fights A16 (six words only).

**Suggestion:** nav stays **Work · Capabilities · Solutions · Perspective · Collective · Connect**.  
The two journeys live in the hero / solutions chapter, not as extra nav items.

## S4. Work = Case Stories

Nav says Work. Content name is Case Stories. Same world.  
URL can be `/work` or `/case-stories` — pick one canonical; the other redirects. Prefer `/work` for humans, title on page: Case Stories.

## S5. Homepage chapter list

A10 is the *job* of the homepage (manifesto + proof + invitation).  
C21 is the *chapter list* (includes Challenge, Process, Loading).

**Follow C21 as the chapter order.** Use A10 as the test: if a chapter doesn’t serve manifesto, proof, or invitation, it doesn’t belong on Home.

## S6. Motion list — keep seven

B11 has Transform. C12 has Collide. Brief says ~5–7, not 8+.

**Seven:** Morph · Draw · Distort · Reveal · Transform · Break · Reassemble  

Collide is a *flavor of Transform* (elements meet → something new), not an eighth style.

## S7. Color — test the three routes; don’t commit early

Brief instinct: monochrome + one signature accent.

If we combine the three *directions* (D01–D03), a coherent test is:

| Mode | When | Field | Accent |
|------|------|--------|--------|
| FUTURE | default world, proof, conversion | near-black + 12-col grid | one warm light (candidate: amber from existing 3D mark — *light*, not a paint bucket) |
| RAW | first 3–8s, morphs, tagline hit | pure black | one electric outline only |
| ART | silence between chapters, Perspective | off-white paper | none — crop is the accent |

That is a **test of combining two**, which the brief allows. It is not a locked palette until we look at it.

Existing `public/brand/` files are material. They do not automatically win Route A/B/C.

## S8. Combining directions (brief allows “one or two”)

If combining rather than picking one:

**FUTURE = spine. RAW = first hit. ART = silence between chapters.**  
Do not average them into one muddy look. Do not ship RAW or ART as the whole site.

**Chosen 2026-08-13.** Now the working visual law. See [DESIGN-SYSTEM.md](./DESIGN-SYSTEM.md).

Keep the investigation list from A17. Ship none of those lines until one of them is true in their words.

That’s enough. Don’t add a fifth.
