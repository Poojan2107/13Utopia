01 — Your brief

The danger

If every offering sits equally — branding, CGI, SEO, SaaS, AI, cloud, CTO advisory — the visitor thinks “13UTOPiA does everything.” You said that is exactly what we must not let them think.

The job

13UTOPiA is a multidisciplinary company that combines imagination, technology and growth to build what conventional companies cannot.

The philosophy

Unreal — imagination, creativity, ambition, possibility.
Unreasonable — challenge assumptions, ambitious engineering, refuse mediocre outcomes.
Make it work — not an art experiment. Brands, products, systems for real businesses.

What you forbade

  • Do not start by making it look impressive. Make the idea impressive first.
  • Do not position as a digital / branding / marketing / software agency.
  • Do not copy Munich, RPA, KOTA, or GSAP. Steal the job they do.
  • Do not design pages before the system.
  • Do not animate everything.
  • Do not promise ranked 1st everywhere.
  • Do not invent Canada or a Why-line that is not true.
  • Do not use Contact us on every button.

Your standard: in 5–10 seconds they know we are not another agency, we can build what we imagine, and we can grow it. WOW and TRUST at once.

05 — Design system (so it doesn’t become random)

Personality

Bold · Intelligent · Playful · Experimental · Precise · Ambitious. Test: playful + precise. Only playful = showreel. Only precise = corporate. Pretty and empty = ART as the whole site. Reject all three.

Visual grammar

Outlines that draw · organic interrupt · type as object · motion that transforms · controlled break of the grid · hidden system (12 columns + 13 forms). Chaos is mathematical.

Seven motions only

Morph · Draw · Distort · Reveal · Transform · Break · Reassemble. Nothing moves without a reason. GSAP + SVG + CSS. 3D only if required.

Type (kit, still a test)

Syne = display (grid in FUTURE, fractured in RAW). Instrument Sans = body, never warped. Instrument Serif = ART only. 3D mark is custom lettering — don’t fake it in a webfont.

Colour — your brief did not name one

Instinct: mostly monochrome + one signature. Kit: void / cream / amber as light.

06 — Three directions, then one law

You asked for three routes, then choose one or combine two. No single route hits 40% technology · 30% studio · 20% editorial · 10% controlled chaos. So we combine jobs, not looks.

RAW

Dark, distorted type, outlines, aggressive motion. 3s: what the hell. 30s risk: art school — unless it snaps to order. Whole site: memorable, hard to sell. Job: first 3–8 seconds, morphs, Normal vs Unreasonable.

FUTURE

Grid, precision, futuristic form, amber as light. 3s: serious. 30s: highest trust — can ship SaaS. Whole site: safest, may lack chaos. Job: default world, proof, conversion.

ART

Editorial, organic curve, cinematic quiet. 3s: taste. 30s: trust via craft; weak BUILD. Whole site: beautiful studio. Job: silence between chapters, Perspective, humanity.

Locked combination: FUTURE = the world they live in · RAW = the first hit · ART = the rest. Do not average into one muddy look.
