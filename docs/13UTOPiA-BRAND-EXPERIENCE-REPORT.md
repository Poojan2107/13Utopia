## 1. What you asked for — in plain language

You gave a long, precise brief. The heart of it is this:

### The danger
If we list every service equally — branding, CGI, SEO, SaaS, AI, cloud, CTO advisory — a visitor will think:

> “13UTOPiA does everything.”

That is exactly what you said we **must not** let them think.

### The job
Communicate this instead:

> **13UTOPiA is a multidisciplinary company that combines imagination, technology and growth to build what conventional companies cannot.**

### The philosophy
**BE UNREAL. BE UNREASONABLE.**

- **Unreal** = imagination, creativity, ambition, possibility.  
- **Unreasonable** = challenge assumptions, ambitious engineering, refuse mediocre outcomes.  
- Invisible third law (from your DNA): **make it work.** We are not an art experiment. We build brands, products, technology and growth systems for real businesses.

### The tension the website must hold
**WOW** (creative, strange, memorable) **and TRUST** (intelligent, precise, commercially mature) at the same time.

A CEO must never wonder: *can these people actually build my SaaS, fix my technology, grow my company, or deliver an enterprise project?*

### What you told us not to do
- Do not start by making it “look impressive.” Make the **idea** impressive first.  
- Do not position as a digital / branding / marketing / software agency.  
- Do not copy Munich, RPA, KOTA, or GSAP showcases. Steal the job they do.  
- Do not design pages before the system.  
- Do not animate everything.  
- Do not promise “ranked 1st everywhere.”  
- Do not invent a Canada story or a “why us” line that is not true.

We treated every one of those as law.

## 6. The design system (so the site does not become random)

Written in full in `docs/strategy/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`. The rules a designer or engineer can actually use:

### Personality
Bold, intelligent, playful, experimental, precise, ambitious.  
The test: **playful + precise.** Only playful = showreel. Only precise = boring corporate.

### Colour (working test — your brief: mostly monochrome + one signature)

You did **not** name a colour. You asked us to test three routes, then pick. Instinct: one unmistakable accent.

What we are testing (not locked as brand law):

| Mode | Field | Accent | Rule |
|------|--------|--------|------|
| FUTURE | Near-black + 12-column grid | Amber as **light** (already in the 3D mark) | Not amber paint on every button |
| RAW | Pure black | One electric outline — *lime was our test, not your brief* | Seconds only. Never a fill. Easy to swap |
| ART | Cream paper | None. Crop is the accent | No second colour |

The existing kit is void / cream / amber light. That feeds FUTURE. Lime is optional RAW ammunition. If you dislike it, RAW can flash white or amber instead. We will not treat lime as the 13UTOPiA brand colour.

### Type (from the kit, still a test)
| Role | Face | Behaviour |
|------|------|-----------|
| Display | Syne | On the grid in FUTURE. Stretched / fractured in RAW |
| Body | Instrument Sans | Always readable. Never warped |
| Editorial | Instrument Serif | ART only — magazine crop |
| Numbers | Instrument Sans | 13 / 07, metrics |

The 3D “13UTOPiA” mark is **custom lettering**. We will not fake it with a Google font.

Sizes: display 120–240px on desktop · body 16–20px · micro 10–13px.  
On mobile: one idea per screen, not 240px shrunk.

### 13 forms (internal — visitors never see the names)
Orbit, Loop, Grid, Wave, Void, Node, Fold, Core, Flow, Frame, Signal, Fragment, Utopia.

They move: idle, cursor, scroll, hover, click. A shape on Home can return, transformed, on an AI page later. That is how the brand is recognised without slapping the logo on every section.

**“13” as mythology — selective.** Load, form IDs, rare `13 / 07`. Not a gimmick on every block.

### Grid
12 columns on desktop. FUTURE lives on it. RAW may ignore it for a few seconds, then **snap** onto it (that snap is the trust moment). ART uses the grid as silence / margins. Breaking the grid is allowed. Staying broken is not.

### Seven motions only
**Morph · Draw · Distort · Reveal · Transform · Break · Reassemble.**

Nothing moves without a reason. No fade-in + slide-up + parallax template.  
Stack: GSAP + ScrollTrigger + SVG + CSS. 3D only when the idea needs it.

### Mobile
Different choreography, not a shrink. Tap instead of cursor. Some decoration dies. The brand stays unreal. It must stay usable.

### SEO (from day one — your correction)
We will not claim “ranked 1st everywhere.”  
We *will* architect: crawlable HTML under the animation, real headings, service URLs only when the page is useful, no thin keyword farms, Organization schema, 13UTOPiA as an **entity**, Core Web Vitals as a design constraint.

*The browser sees a great website. The visitor sees a completely unreasonable website.*
