## 4. SEO & AEO writing rules

1. **One primary topic per URL** — H1 matches the page promise.  
2. **Answer first** — first 40–60 words state what it is, who it’s for, what you get.  
3. **Unique title + meta** every leaf (no duplicate “Digital Marketing Agency Ahmedabad” spam).  
4. **Natural local signal** — Ahmedabad / India HQ where true; don’t keyword-stuff.  
5. **FAQ blocks** on Connect + complex Capability leaves (2–5 Qs, plain answers).  
6. **No mythology keywords** in titles/H1s (Zeus, Poseidon, Atlas, etc.).  
7. **Internal links** — each leaf → pillar → Solutions that use it → Connect.

### Title pattern
`{Service or page} | 13UTOPiA`  
or `{Outcome} — {short proof} | 13UTOPiA`

### Meta description pattern (~150–160 chars)
What you do + for whom + outcome + soft CTA. No gods language.
